Runner Procedural

Unity3D development implementing real-time procedural audio using additive synthesis, wavetable synthesis and FM synthesis.

In this demo you control a runner where audio is generated dynamically in real time according to interaction, movement and in-game events. The experience showcases how different procedural synthesis techniques reshape the sound response of the game instead of relying only on pre-recorded clips.
